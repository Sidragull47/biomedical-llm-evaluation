# Biology Reasoning Case 01

## Topic
RNA-seq interpretation and scientific overclaiming

## Prompt

Microglial RNA-seq performed one day after sensory deprivation shows
significant enrichment of inflammatory-response pathways compared with
baseline.

Interpret this result. Clearly distinguish direct observations from
biological hypotheses, avoid unsupported causal conclusions, and
identify one additional experiment needed to determine whether
neuronal damage occurred.

## Reference Answer

The RNA-seq result provides evidence that inflammatory-response
transcriptional programs are altered or enriched in microglia after
one day of sensory deprivation.

This finding may be consistent with a change in microglial immune
state, but RNA-seq alone does not establish functional
neuroinflammation, neuronal damage, or a causal relationship between
microglial activity and neuronal injury.

Additional experiments would be required to test neuronal damage,
such as appropriate histological or biochemical measurements of
neuronal injury.

## Example Model Response

"Sensory deprivation activates microglia and causes neuroinflammation.
The activated microglia subsequently damage neurons in the visual
cortex."

## Expert Evaluation

The response overinterprets the RNA-seq evidence.

The enrichment of inflammatory-response pathways supports a change
in inflammatory-related transcriptional programs, but it does not
by itself demonstrate functional neuroinflammation.

The statement that activated microglia damage neurons is unsupported
because no measurement of neuronal injury was provided.

Additional experiments would be required to establish neuronal
damage and any causal relationship between microglial changes and
neuronal injury.
